2. Visiting the website and server log files

When you visit this website, the hosting provider’s server processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page accessed and information about the browser used. This is necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its secure operation. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

3. No cookies, no tracking, no external services

This website does not set cookies and does not use any analytics or tracking tools. Fonts and images are loaded directly from our server. No content from third parties such as Google Fonts, social media plugins or video platforms is embedded.

We only refer to our Instagram profiles with simple links, not with embedded content. Only when you click one of these links does Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) open; from then on Instagram’s privacy policy applies.

4. Enquiries by email, phone and enquiry form

The enquiry form on the home page does not transmit any data itself. It only creates an email in your own email program. Only when you send this email do we receive the information it contains, i.e. the package you are interested in, your name, your phone number and message if given, and your email address. The same applies if you email us directly or call us.

We use this information to answer your enquiry and to prepare your participation in a course. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) and, for general enquiries, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering). We delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Our email account is hosted by Google (Gmail). Emails to us are therefore processed on Google’s servers. A transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. Booking and payment

Payment via Stripe

If you buy a package using the “Book now” button, you are redirected to a Stripe payment page. The provider is Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland. Stripe processes the information you enter there, in particular your name, email address, billing address, payment details and your consent to the immediate start of the online course, and passes on to us the data needed to process the order (without full card or account details).

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR (retention obligations under tax and commercial law). Stripe may also transfer data to the USA; Stripe, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Depending on the payment method chosen (for example PayPal or Klarna), Stripe passes data on to the respective payment provider. More information: Stripe privacy policy.

The payment page belongs to Stripe; Stripe sets its own cookies there, among other things to prevent fraud. On this website itself, the information in section 3 still applies.

Course platform

Booking, payment and access to the online course do not take place via this website but via an external course platform.

To exercise these rights, please use the contact details above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example with the authority responsible for us, the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany.
