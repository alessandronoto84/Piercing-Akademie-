How you learn

Each module builds on the previous one

  1. ExplanationContent firstKey points, tables, photos from the course book and graphics drawn especially for the course.
  2. QuestionsThen the questionsOne at a time, with instant feedback. Wrong answers come back.
  3. NextThen the next lessonIt only opens once you have mastered all the questions.
  4. ExamModule exam20 random questions, pass mark 80 percent. For hygiene and emergencies, 85 percent.
  5. TrainingMistake trainingEverything you got wrong comes back until you have answered it correctly twice.
  6. FinalMock final exam50 questions from all modules. For hygiene and emergencies, at most one mistake is allowed.

From the lessons

Practice lab: practical exercises with a template and self-check, plus short clips showing how piercing is done. With the Complete package you also get a practice kit with a silicone ear and pierce it yourself. So you arrive well prepared for your practice day.

The online course does not replace hands-on supervision. It teaches the knowledge. You only pierce on the practice day, under supervision. Do not pierce anyone on your own after the online course.
