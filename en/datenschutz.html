Privacy policy

This English version is a translation for your information. Only the German version is legally binding.

Here you can find out which personal data is processed when you visit this website and when you send us an inquiry. This policy describes the actual technical state of the website.

2. Visiting the website and server log files

When you visit this website, the hosting provider’s server processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page accessed and information about the browser used. This is necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its secure operation. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

The hosting provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. We have a data processing agreement with Cloudflare; Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Server log data is stored only briefly.

3. No tracking cookies, no tracking, no external services

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not use any analytics or tracking tools. Only when you log in to the online course do we set a technically necessary session cookie (see section 5). Fonts and images are loaded directly from our server. No content from third parties such as Google Fonts, social media plugins or video platforms is embedded.

We only refer to our Instagram profiles with simple links, not with embedded content. Only when you click one of these links does Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) open; from then on Instagram’s privacy policy applies.

4. Inquiries by email, phone and enquiry form

When you submit the enquiry form for the Practice or Complete package, we transmit your details (package, name, email address, phone number, preferred dates for the practice day and the first phone call, time and, if given, your message) via our server at Cloudflare to us by email and send you a confirmation of receipt; sending is handled by the service Resend (see section 5). We store the enquiry in our database at Cloudflare so that, after the phone call, we can send you the confirmation of your date with a payment link. If no booking results, we delete it after 6 months, otherwise after 3 years. To protect against mass spam, we store only the time and a non-reversible hash of your IP address for one day. The same applies accordingly if you email or call us directly.

We use this information to answer your inquiry and to prepare your participation in a course. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) and, for general inquiries, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering). We delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Our email account is hosted by Google (Gmail). Emails to us are therefore processed on Google’s servers. A transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. Booking and payment

Payment via Stripe

If you buy a package using the “Book now” button, you are redirected to a Stripe payment page. The provider is Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland. Stripe processes the information you enter there, in particular your name, email address, billing address, payment details and your consent to the immediate start of the online course, and passes on to us the data needed to process the order (without full card or account details).

When you click “Order with obligation to pay” on the order page, we store the chosen package, the language and the time and version of your two confirmations (terms, immediate start of the online course) as proof, without your name, email or IP address. After payment, the entry is linked to your purchase. If no payment is made, we delete it after 30 days, otherwise after 3 years. To prevent misuse, we also store a hash of your IP address for one day (as in section 4). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR.

When you book the Complete package, Stripe asks for your delivery address for the practice set. We receive it by email, use it only for shipping and pass your name and address on to the delivery company for this purpose (e.g. DHL or Deutsche Post). With the digital course book (also in the course packages) we display every page with your email address as a watermark (protection against unauthorized sharing, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). To prevent mass retrieval, we store a hash and the time of each page view for one day. We store your notes on the book together with your course progress and delete them with your access. To prevent payments from being processed twice, we store the Stripe session ID, the product, your email address and the amount for each purchase of the digital course book for three years. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR (retention obligations under tax and commercial law). Stripe may also transfer data to the USA; Stripe, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Depending on the payment method chosen (for example PayPal or Klarna), Stripe passes data on to the respective payment provider. More information: Stripe privacy policy.

The payment page belongs to Stripe; Stripe sets its own cookies there, among other things to prevent fraud. On this website itself, the information in section 3 still applies.

Access to the online course

After your purchase we create course access for you. For this we store your email address, name, booked package, language, date of purchase, amount and your consent to the immediate start of the course, as well as your learning progress (completed lessons, exam results) and, if applicable, your certificate. You log in with a one-time code that we send by email. After logging in, your browser stores a technically necessary session cookie (valid for 30 days), without which the course cannot be used.

Because access is personal, we record every login with the time, device type (for example “iOS · Safari”) and country; we do not store the IP address. If an unusually high number of different devices is noticed, we check for possible sharing. The course pages show your email address as a watermark for identification.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and, for the login record, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in protecting the course content against unauthorized sharing). We delete course data twelve months after your access expires and the login record after 90 days; invoice data is kept for the statutory periods. The data is stored in a database at Cloudflare (see section 2).

We send the emails with login codes and purchase confirmations via the service Resend (Resend, Inc., USA) as a processor. Your email address and the content of the email are processed; any transfer to the USA is based on standard contractual clauses.

Electronic withdrawal

If you withdraw from a contract using the “Withdraw from contract” page, we store your name, email address, the contract concerned, the booking date, your note and the time of receipt, send you a confirmation of receipt and notify ourselves by email (sent via Resend). The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR (statutory obligation to confirm receipt, Section 356a BGB) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (proof of the withdrawal). We delete this data after three years.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) and to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR). To exercise these rights, please use the contact details above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example with the authority responsible for us, the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany.
