Cancellation policy · Model withdrawal form

§ 1 Scope

These general terms and conditions apply to all contracts for the course packages of the Piercing Akademie (Basic, Practice, Complete) and for the purchase of the digital course book (read online) between 740 Piercing, owner Alessandro Noto, Burgstraße 12, 49808 Lingen (Ems), Germany (hereinafter “Academy”), and its participants (hereinafter “Customer”). They apply equally to consumers and businesses unless expressly stated otherwise. Deviating terms of the Customer only apply if the Academy expressly agrees to them in text form.

§ 2 Conclusion of contract

The presentation of the packages on the website is not a binding offer but a non-binding invitation to make an offer. No contract is concluded via the website itself: the inquiry form only creates an email to the Academy. If the Customer books a package using the “Book now” button, they are taken to the order page. There they see the package, the services and the total price and can still change the package. They confirm these terms and, for the online course, the immediate start (Section 4), and by clicking “Order with obligation to pay” they make a binding offer. They are then forwarded to the payment page of the payment service provider Stripe, where they enter their email address, billing address and payment method and can correct them before paying. The contract is concluded when the payment is successfully completed; if the Customer cancels the payment, no contract is concluded. The contract is also concluded if the Customer makes a binding booking of the chosen package via the Academy’s course platform and the Academy confirms the booking or activates the service. If the Academy and the Customer agree the booking directly (for example by email), the contract is concluded with the Academy’s confirmation in text form. The Customer receives a confirmation of the contract together with these terms in text form.

§ 3 Requirements for participation

Participants must be at least 18 years old. No previous knowledge is required. For the practice days the Customer must be in good enough health to work on a model under hygienic conditions and must follow the studio’s hygiene and safety rules. Practice days and coaching are held in German or English.

§ 4 Services

The content and scope of the packages are set out in the description of services on the website in the version valid at the time of booking:

  • Basic: access to the online course with 11 modules, quiz questions, checklists and worksheets, final test, certificate of participation and the course book (online, in an English translation with the German original pages) and practice tasks for every module with a materials list. In the Basic and Practice packages, the Customer obtains the practice materials for home themselves.
  • Practice: all services of Basic, plus one practice day in the studio in Lingen working on a model under supervision.
  • Complete: all services of Practice, plus 1:1 coaching with personal feedback, a second practice day, a practice set for home with the materials for the practice tasks (shipped to the delivery address in Germany or Austria given at checkout) and 12 months of support after the course. Places are limited.

Available separately:

  • Course book digital: reading access to “Die Piercing Akademie – Dein Weg zum Bodypiercer” in the online course for 24 months, as an English translation with the German original pages, with full-text search and personal notes; no download file is provided. Access is personal and not transferable, every page carries the customer’s identifier; copying, passing on or reproducing it is not permitted. The course book is included in all course packages.

Access to the online course is granted for 12 months from activation. The dates of the practice days are agreed together after booking.

Immediate start and right of withdrawal for the online course: The online course is sold only with an immediate start: the Academy activates access immediately after receipt of payment. A booking of the online course is therefore only possible if the Customer expressly agrees during checkout that the Academy will begin providing the course before the withdrawal period has expired, and confirms that they thereby lose their right of withdrawal for the online course. When access is activated (first access to the course), the right of withdrawal for the online course expires (Section 356 (5) German Civil Code, BGB); from then on the purchase of the online course is final. The Academy confirms the contract, including this consent and acknowledgement, to the Customer in text form. The cancellation policy below applies to practice days, coaching and the course book. Customers who book as a business (Section 14 BGB) have no statutory right of withdrawal.

§ 5 Models on the practice day

The Customer may bring their own models. Models must be adults and must sign a consent form before being pierced. If the Customer has no model, the Academy looks for models for the practice day. This usually works out; however, the Academy cannot guarantee a particular number of models or particular types of piercing. If no model can be found for an agreed date, the Academy offers an alternative date.

§ 6 Prices and payment

The prices in euros stated at the time of booking apply. All prices are final prices including statutory VAT. The available payment methods are shown during the booking process. Payments are processed by the payment service provider Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Dublin, Ireland); its terms for the chosen payment method apply in addition. Unless otherwise agreed, the price is due when the contract is concluded and before the service begins.

§ 7 Rescheduling and cancellation by the Customer

An agreed practice day or coaching session can be moved once free of charge to another available date up to 14 days before the date. In the event of a later cancellation or if the Customer does not attend, the Academy will try to find an alternative date. If no alternative date can be arranged, the Academy remains entitled to payment; however, it must deduct what it saves as a result of the cancellation or earns by giving the place to someone else (Section 615 BGB). The statutory right of withdrawal for consumers remains unaffected.

§ 8 Cancellation by the Academy

If the Academy has to cancel a date for good reason, for example because the instructor is ill, the Customer is informed without delay and receives an alternative date. If an alternative date cannot reasonably be expected of the Customer, the Academy refunds the part of the price attributable to the canceled service. Further claims exist only in accordance with § 11.

§ 9 Course content and rights of use

The online course, course book, checklists and worksheets are protected by copyright. The Customer may use them only for their own training. Passing on login details, copying, publishing or passing on to third parties is not permitted without the Academy’s prior written consent.

Access to the online course is personal and non-transferable. It is tied to the email address given at purchase; you log in with a one-time code sent to this address. The online course can be used on at most two devices at the same time; logging in on a further device ends the oldest login. To protect against sharing, course content, templates and the certificate are marked with the Customer’s name or email address, and logins are recorded (time, device type). If the Customer passes on their access or distributes course content, the Academy may block access after a prior warning, or without a warning in the case of serious breaches. In this case there is no claim to a refund of the price of the online course; further claims of the Academy are reserved.

§ 10 Certificate of participation

After completing the course content, the Customer receives a certificate of participation from the Piercing Akademie. It confirms participation and training at the Academy. It is not a state-recognized qualification and not an official permit. Whether and under what conditions the Customer may offer piercings commercially is decided by the competent authorities.

§ 11 Liability

The Academy is liable without limitation for intent and gross negligence, for injury to life, body or health and under the German Product Liability Act. In the case of slightly negligent breach of essential contractual obligations, i.e. obligations whose fulfilment makes the proper performance of the contract possible in the first place and on whose fulfilment the Customer may regularly rely, liability is limited to the foreseeable damage typical for the contract. Otherwise, liability for slight negligence is excluded. The Customer alone is responsible for piercings they perform on their own responsibility after the course.

§ 12 Data protection

The privacy policy applies to the processing of personal data.

§ 13 Final provisions

The law of the Federal Republic of Germany applies, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. For consumers, this choice of law applies only insofar as it does not deprive them of the protection of mandatory provisions of the country in which they have their habitual residence. If the Customer is a merchant, a legal entity under public law or a special fund under public law, the place of jurisdiction is Lingen (Ems). Should any provision of these terms be invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions remains unaffected.

Cancellation policy

Consumers have a right of withdrawal as set out below. A consumer is any natural person who concludes a legal transaction for purposes that are predominantly outside their trade, business or profession (Section 13 BGB).

Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the day the contract is concluded. For the practice set included in the Complete package (goods), it is fourteen days from the day on which you, or a third party other than the carrier indicated by you, acquire physical possession of the goods.

To exercise your right of withdrawal, you must inform us (740 Piercing, owner Alessandro Noto, Burgstraße 12, 49808 Lingen (Ems), Germany, phone: +49 173 8072405, email: diepiercingakademie@gmail.com) of your decision to withdraw from this contract by means of a clear statement (e.g. a letter sent by mail or an email). You may use the attached model withdrawal form, but this is not obligatory.

If you concluded the contract via our website, you can also withdraw using the “Withdraw from contract” button on our website (withdraw from contract). If you do so, we will send you confirmation of receipt of your withdrawal by email without delay.

To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient for you to send your communication concerning your exercise of the right of withdrawal before the withdrawal period has expired.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse to you all payments received from you, including the costs of delivery (with the exception of the supplementary costs resulting from your choice of a type of delivery other than the least expensive type of standard delivery offered by us), without undue delay and in any event not later than fourteen days from the day on which we are informed about your decision to withdraw from this contract. We will carry out such reimbursement using the same means of payment as you used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly agreed otherwise; in any event, you will not incur any fees as a result of such reimbursement.

If you have received goods (the practice set from the Complete package or a printed course book), you shall send them back or hand it over to us without undue delay and in any event not later than fourteen days from the day on which you communicate your withdrawal from this contract to us. The deadline is met if you send the goods back before the period of fourteen days has expired. You will have to bear the direct cost of returning the goods. We may withhold reimbursement until we have received the goods back or you have supplied evidence of having sent it back, whichever is the earliest. You are only liable for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling other than what is necessary to establish its nature, characteristics and functioning.

If you requested that the services (practice days, coaching) begin during the withdrawal period, you shall pay us an amount which is in proportion to what has been provided until you have communicated to us your withdrawal from this contract, in comparison with the full coverage of the contract.

Special note on the online course and the digital course book

In the case of a contract for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium (online course), your right of withdrawal expires once we have begun providing it (activation of your access or activation of reading access to the course book), after you have expressly consented to us beginning before the end of the withdrawal period, you have acknowledged that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal once provision begins, and we have provided you with a confirmation of the contract (Section 356 (5) BGB).

Special note on the practice set (Complete package)

The right of withdrawal expires early for contracts for the supply of sealed goods which are not suitable for return due to health protection or hygiene reasons, if their seal has been removed after delivery. This applies to the sterile-packed parts of the practice set, for example cannulas and disposable gloves.
