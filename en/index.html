The practice day

A day in the studio in Lingen

You do as many different piercings as possible, always under supervision. Bring your own models, or the studio looks for some for you. We cannot guarantee this, though.

● Dates by arrangement · in German or English

Safety first. Every model is an adult and signs a consent form in advance. We work according to the studio’s hygiene plan.
Knowing the technique is not the same as supervised practice. The online course teaches the knowledge; you only pierce here, under supervision. Do not pierce anyone on your own after the online course.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any prior knowledge?
No. The course starts with the basics. If you already work in a studio, you can focus on what you need.
How do I get access to the online course?
After booking you receive your login details by email. You learn at your own pace on your phone, tablet or computer.
Can I bring my own model to the practice day?
Yes. The model must be an adult and signs a consent form in advance. If you have no model, the studio looks for one for you. This usually works out, but we cannot guarantee it.
When are the practice days?
By arrangement. Tell us when suits you and we will find a date together. Practice days take place in German or English.
Can I open a studio after the course?
That is decided by your local authorities. Module 11 shows what to prepare in Germany; in other countries the local rules apply.
What is the difference between Practice and Complete?
Complete also includes 1:1 coaching, a second practice day, practice material for home and one year of support.
